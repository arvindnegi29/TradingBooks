## Foreword and introduction (12-44)
Farley's foreword12-14 emphasizes matching activity to available time, knowing exits before entry, bounded size, patience and selecting entries rather than chasing. It also claims a mathematical trading advantage over investing and eventual success; neither follows from the evidence supplied and neither is adopted.
Carr's introduction20-44 narrates early paper/real trades, tips/news speculation, losses, move toward technical systems and later regime dependence. These are anecdotes and self-reported historical performance, not an audited ledger or a baseline comparison.
PDF23-26: Darvas-box and AIM summaries describe other authors' methods; reading this summary is not independent reading/validation of their books. Do not import AIM averaging or loosen a risk plan because a named method is mentioned.
PDF27-31: rumor-driven purchase ramps1,000shares at2,2.50,3, costing7,500 funded with5,000cash plus2,500borrowed. Exit1.38 returns4,140, gross loss3,360 before commissions, equity1,640 after loan principal. Fills can worsen greatly beyond intended level. This illustrates news identity error, margin exposure and latency, not a recommendation to trade rumors or take that size.
PDF33-37: source advocates technical-only entries after fundamental/tip failures. Do not turn this into suppressing scheduled-event, earnings, corporate-action or liquidity checks. Price-derived indicators and candles remain correlated, not four independent evidential votes. Source says technicals reflect all available business information; a price path does not reveal all future facts or actor intent.
PDF38-42: BTTT-MAX hourly MA crossover narrative was always invested and reversed direction, with spectacular2000returns followed by degradation when conditions changed. The author explicitly says the edge depended on regime. No exact MA pair, complete executions, costs, selection history or heldout performance is supplied here; no implementation or profitable edge follows. Never adopt always-in-market or automatic reversal in this manual-only skill.
PDF42-44: commercial services, accounts, author affiliations and technology are historical; biography/self-promotion cannot validate current performance. Claims anyone can trade for a living or systems are nearly100%mechanical do not establish feasibility, sufficient capital or testable rules. Subsequent chapters still contain judgment and source inconsistencies.

## Chapter1: infrastructure (48-56; blank57)
Core idea: choose reliable tools appropriate to required observations and execution. Software/hardware cannot create an edge. The claim of90%+profit margins is unsupported and ignores market losses/risk.
PDF49-50: RAM500MB/1GB, monitors/prices and Windows/Mac claims are dated2007-08 observations, not current buying advice.
PDF51-54: historical free20minute delayed charts, named services and fee schedules are not current provider specifications. Delayed chart data may support study but cannot establish a live entry; keep current exchange timestamps and executable quotes/freshness gates. Software lineups and subscriptions are not prerequisites or reason to reconnect existing providers.
PDF54-56: backup connectivity and comfort with order-entry platform are useful process ideas. Historical dial-up/mobile recommendations, broker names, commissions and account budgets are not current facts. No install, credential, permission, provider change or trade is authorized by a book.
## Integration boundary
Use anecdote/regime/cost lessons only with exact provenance. Preserve actual user budget, causal setup rules, fresh venue data, applicable session/contracts and manual execution. Ignore guaranteed-income, superiority and faith/charity-as-profit-cause assertions. No live market analysis or strategy validation was performed by this reader.
