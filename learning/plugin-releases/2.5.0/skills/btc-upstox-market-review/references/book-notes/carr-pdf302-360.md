## Chapter15 neutral options: source propositions, not validated edge

PDF302–304 / printed283–285 introduces long earnings straddles and asserts approximately 10–20% short-hold returns, around 60% winners if rules followed, small typical losses, and 50%+ for legged variants. These are unsupported performance assertions in the chapter, without sample size, trade ledger, complete losing trades, transaction-cost methodology, volatility data, or out-of-sample tests. Description of the position as simultaneously long and short on PDF303 is misleading: both options are purchased; only directional exposures oppose one another.

PDF303–311 /284–292 screen for after-close earnings three market days ahead, liquid optionable high-beta stocks, price above $40, volume at least one million shares, beta at least2, and gaps above $1 at the preceding two earnings events. Calendar tables are historical examples, not contemporary data. Equity share volume does not establish option liquidity. Absolute price/gap and beta cutoffs are US-equity heuristics requiring independent validation elsewhere. Screen says discard missing announcement times, but reduced list PDF306 includes BDN whose Table15.2 PDF308 says time not supplied. It also excludes explicitly after-close 4:15pm APTI (PDF307) and 5pm CJRb.TO (PDF310), illustrating brittle treatment of equivalent time labels. PDF306 labels examples July22,2006 while charts show 2004; July22,2006 was Saturday (calendar arithmetic), inconsistent with the stated weekday earnings framework. Do not silently normalize source chronology.

PDF312–315 /293–296 uses AMZN/BRCM historical earnings chart gaps and reversals as favorable illustrations. Calls these easy or huge winners, but stock-price movement alone cannot establish long-option P&L without entry debit, implied volatility, expiry, bid/ask, and exit fills. BRCM figures below $40 do not visually demonstrate compliance with $40 threshold at the hypothetical July entry.

PDF315–319 /296–300: two of three oversold/overbought readings authorize an initial directional leg three days before earnings. RSI5 below30/above70; stochastic%K5 below25/above75; CCI20 below−100/above+100. Oversold buys call first, overbought put first; acquire opposite leg on earnings day. Without signal buy both earnings day. Source favors same nearest strike, though PDF317 allows nearest separate strikes if spot between strikes; these can produce a different structure rather than one same-strike straddle. Both acquired calls and puts should have specified equal expiry/quantity/multiplier to calculate risk. Chapter12 is cross-referenced for expiry choice; not inferred here. A legged entry is directional until second leg exists and may pay a different strike/volatility/debit later. Early legging cannot be classified market-neutral throughout.

PDF317–320 /298–301 states upside unlimited, downside two paid premiums, no margin, time-decay cost. Correct theoretical expiry P&L for equal-quantity, same-strike K long call+put is |S_T−K|−D per underlying unit, D=C+P; max loss D at K; upper break-even K+D, lower K−D only if nonnegative/attainable. Upside unbounded on rally; downside terminal gain bounded by K−D as underlying approaches0. Fees enlarge loss/debit. The book's break-even entry PDF318 says trade remains near break-even less spread/time decay and is not held to expiry. That is not a valid break-even formula and materially understates volatility repricing risk. A move must exceed total debit at expiry; before expiry option prices depend on remaining time and IV as well as spot. Long gamma does not guarantee that winning-leg gains exceed losing-leg loss after earnings because IV can collapse. PDF320 delta explanation is incomplete; no 60%/small-loss promise should survive into operational guidance.

PDF321–324 /302–305 exits: for legged entries with >$1 gap, dispose losing leg at next open and retain winning leg while candle agrees, capped at fifth trading day after earnings. For simultaneous entry with >$1 gap, source instead disposes winner first, retaining loser to pursue reversal (PDF322–323). With <$0.50 gap, oversold retains call, overbought retains put; neither retains winner. $0.50–$1 uses judgment, generally same rules. These are source rules with significant directional/reversal exposure after first exit, not a continuing neutral hedge. Rules do not fully define intraday handling, exact first contrary-candle execution, quote slippage at opening, or mark used for identifying winner. A 5-day clock must be tested against expiry.

PDF324 /305 arithmetic: 10% profit on one leg plus100% on other yields55% total return ONLY if original cash outlays equal; correct weighted result is (0.10*C1+1.00*C2)/(C1+C2). Equal contract counts do not imply equal premiums, especially with entries days apart. Cost-adjusted result lower. It is a hypothetical favorable path, not observed expectancy. Claimed window12–16 weeks/year and small-loss claims are not empirical proof. Holding descriptions vary: PDF3022–5days, PDF303 up to seven in all, PDF324 eight trading days (three before plus five after); document exact counting rather than make a performance claim.

PDF325–327 /306–308 consolidates screen and warns options should follow demonstrated profitable stock trading because spread, time decay and changing delta can accelerate failure. Retain the educational caution as the author's view; prior stock success cannot itself validate an options edge.

## Transfer limits for BTC and Indian markets

This source chapter describes US stocks/options and US earnings calendar/session conventions circa2004–2008. BTC has no company quarterly earnings event or corresponding overnight market-close structure; direct transplant of after-close earnings rules, $1/$40 thresholds, SP500 beta, share-volume cutoffs or NY option hours is invalid. For Indian equity/indices or BTC options, independently obtain current contract multiplier/lot, exercise style, cash/physical settlement, expiry schedule, trading hours, strike increments, margin and charges from venue documentation before applying any payoff assumption. India/BTC venue/provider data and executable option bid/ask depth, spread, IV/skew, total debit, event expectation, and expiry define actual exposure. All guidance remains conditional educational manual planning; no live recommendation or proven edge follows from this historical chapter. Source ideas can become explicit hypotheses for an independently requested backtest with costs, complete samples and held-out periods; they are not prevalidated parameters.
