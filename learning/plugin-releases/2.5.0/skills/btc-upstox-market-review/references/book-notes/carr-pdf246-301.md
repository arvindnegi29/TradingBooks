## Execution and risk framework, with source scope
- PDF246–248 / pp227–229: qualify setup, screening and risk/reward first; author enters longs above previous-session high, shorts below previous-session low, using historical US stock buffers $0.02 or $0.05. A gap through the trigger prompts a retracement limit order or abandonment. Text subsequently also endorses paying up late and adjusting stop/target: this is discretionary tension, not a coherent chase rule. Preserve pre-entry re-evaluation and skip logic; do not import dollar buffers or permission to chase BTC/Indian instruments.
- PDF249–253 / pp230–234: author requires an exact adverse exit level and offers percentage, structural support/resistance, 2×ATR(14), and parabolic SAR alternatives. Structural invalidation should derive from chart evidence. SAR is described as vulnerable to chop; chart PDF254 confirms repeated flips. Stops are planned triggers, not guaranteed fill prices or loss ceilings. ATR is in underlying price units, not percentage or option premium units.
- Historical percentage parameters are 8% maximum stock-price decline, often 5–6%; these are NOT account-risk percentages and must not become plugin defaults. Text's high win rates, newsletter loss averages, and best-stop assertions are unsupported performance claims.
- PDF254–256 / pp235–237: alternatives for taking profit are 1.5–2×initial stop distance, opposing structure, 3×ATR against a 2×ATR stop, or trailing on favorable closes. Do not combine incompatible fixed-target and open-ended trailing policies silently. Tightening should not become stop widening. RIMM arithmetic visually checked: entry96, ATR3.25 → stop89.50, target105.75; risk6.50, reward9.75, ratio1.5 before costs. 6.50/96=6.7708%, target gain10.15625%.
- PDF257–258 / pp238–239: practice chart recognition and explicitly think about position sizing. Equal allocations/dividing by10 and win/loss streak resizing are the author's historical suggestions, not verified optimal sizing. Position value is distinct from cash at risk; total portfolio dependence matters.

## Options mechanics and author rules
- PDF262–281 / pp243–262: rights versus obligations; calls/puts; opening versus closing transactions; American/European exercise; moneyness; premium composed of intrinsic and extrinsic value; signed local sensitivity delta; expiry and liquidity. Historical US equity example uses 100 underlying shares per contract and dollar-per-share premiums. These units are venue-specific.
- PDF282–287 / pp263–268: author prefers more time to expiry, liquid underlying and narrow option spreads, near ATM/ITM strikes, smaller option allocations, faster profits, alert-based adverse exits rather than resting stops, limit profit orders, protection of a formerly large gain near breakeven, and rolling profitable positions before expiry. These are historical proposals for US stock swing options; do not import exact thresholds or blanket order/expiry policies.
- Numerically the source says at least one month to expiry except day trades; next month for <2week expected holding and at least two months out for longer holds; underlying daily shares >=1million; spread<=10% of premium, with 0.10/0.30 dollar heuristics; 50% cash reserve and ~10% allocations if options-only or20% total options with mixed assets; 25–40% premium adverse alert; never exceed40% position loss or5% account loss; 50% favorable mark then breakeven protection; roll two weeks before expiry. Record only as provenance, never defaults or user risk consent.
- PDF286 / p267 KLAC calculation: starting premium5, underlying move5, delta0.5 implies approximate premium7.5, +50%; it is first-order repricing, not a realizable target guarantee. Delta/gamma, implied volatility, time decay, spreads and fees can alter result.
- PDF287 / p268 author instructs holding compound positions to expiry (straddle excepted) and permits pyramiding profitable rolls. Neither can safely become an unconditional rule: expiry settlement, assignment, funding, leg liquidity and risk budget must be checked.
- PDF290–300 / pp271–281 choose structures by directional thesis and exposure limits. Confidence does not justify unlimited exposure. Long calls, long puts, naked writes, covered calls, bullish and bearish verticals are described. Explicit distinguish premium outlay, bounded expiry payoff, actual execution and assignment exposure.

## Independent audit: source errors and overclaims
The following are in the printed source, not just extraction problems; key formula errors visually confirmed where listed.
1. PDF264 / p245 ATM glossary compares underlying price with itself; correct reference is strike versus underlying.
2. PDF270 / p251 assignment summary wrongly labels seller obligation with buyer words. A short call obligates delivery/sale; a short put obligates purchase, subject to contract settlement style.
3. PDF280 / p261 says short puts benefit from falling/flat prices. Falling prices increase put liability; directional exposure of a short put is bullish. Visually confirmed.
4. PDF281 / p262 EBAY S40, K35 call at8 is decomposed as intrinsic3/time5. Correct: intrinsic5, extrinsic3. Visually confirmed. Their OTM K45 example intrinsic0/extrinsic4 is correct.
5. PDF284 / p265 nearest-strike example assigns same strike for calls and puts while claiming ITM: S27 K27.5 call is OTM and put ITM; S26 K25 call ITM and put OTM. Selection must be type-aware.
6. PDF292 / p273 long-call breakeven omits premium (says strike plus spread/commission). Correct expiry BE K+c+per-unit costs. Visually confirmed.
7. PDF293 / p274 short-put BE needs K−p (then costs adjustment), not unspecified spread; finite worst-case loss per unit K−p if underlying floors at0, not literally unlimited.
8. PDF294 / p275 purported bull-put implementation buys ITM higher-strike put and sells OTM lower-strike put, which is a bearish debit put spread. Correct bull-put is short higher-strike put plus long lower-strike put for credit. Source stated profit/loss limits correspond to the corrected bullish credit structure, not its written legs. Visually confirmed.
9. PDF297 / p278 long-put upside is finite for nonnegative underlying: K−p per unit, and BE K−p, not strike plus spread. Visually confirmed.
10. PDF298 / p279 short-call BE K+c per unit before costs, not K−spread; bearish spread rationale accidentally describes rising price. Visually confirmed.
11. PDF299 / p280 purported bear-put legs sell higher ITM put/buy lower OTM put (bullish credit structure), while claiming debit. Correct bear-put buys higher put and sells lower put for debit. Its maximum gain occurs below lower strike, not above higher strike; maximum loss above higher is correct for corrected legs. Visually confirmed through PDF300 / p281.
12. PDF277 / p258 decay chart is schematic: x=time-to-expiry months9,6,3,0; y=percent premium remaining100,90,60,0. It is not a universal decay calibration or proof that every option decays exponentially. Extrinsic decay varies with moneyness, volatility and time; total premium also responds to underlying/IV. No quantitative backtest should derive from curve.
13. PDF273–275 / pp254–256 EBAY example: five calls at4 cost2000, sold10 realize3000 =150%; stock500×40 costs20000 and move50 yields5000=25%; correct arithmetic under assumed marks. Fifty calls yield30000 profit, six times stock profit. Calling this 600% leverage conflates return/profit comparisons with delta-adjusted exposure. Downside call4→2 is hypothetical, not implied by stock40→30. Same-dollar option investing may lose100%, versus example stock25%. Claim options necessarily less risky is unsupported.
14. PDF286 / p267 claim a5% account loss guarantees20 trades confuses fixed initial dollars and current-equity fractions;20 successive5% current-equity losses leave0.95^20≈35.85%, not zero; fill gaps also invalidate guaranteed loss caps.
15. Historical third-Friday monthly expiry, fixed strike intervals, 100-share contracts, no extended options trading, universal writer margin, stock uptick/borrow rules, and broker/IRA restrictions are not portable current specifications. Covered calls buffer only premium and retain large stock downside; they are not equivalent to protective puts. Straddle gamma does not ensure earnings profit after premium, implied-volatility contraction and costs.

## Corrected payoff reference for adaptation (own derivation)
Let S be settlement underlying, K strike, c/p entry call/put premium per underlying unit; same-expiry vertical Klow<Khigh, widthW=Khigh−Klow, net debitD or creditC; multiplierM and quantityQ known. Before fees:
- Long call: max(S−K,0)−c; BE K+c; maxloss c.
- Long put: max(K−S,0)−p; BE K−p; maxgain K−p for S>=0; maxloss p.
- Short call: c−max(S−K,0); BE K+c; maxgain c; unbounded loss for unbounded S.
- Short put: p−max(K−S,0); BE K−p; maxgain p; maxloss K−p if S>=0.
- Bull call (buy low call, sell high call): maxgainW−D, maxlossD, BE Klow+D.
- Bull put (sell high put, buy low put): maxgainC, maxlossW−C, BE Khigh−C.
- Bear call (sell low call, buy high call): maxgainC, maxlossW−C, BE Klow+C.
- Bear put (buy high put, sell low put): maxgainW−D, maxlossD, BE Khigh−D.
Cash results = per-unit result×M×Q, provided quote and settlement units align; fees and delivery/collateral liabilities are additional. These are expiry payoffs, not pre-expiry MTM guarantees. Matched European cash-settled expiry assumptions cannot be silently extended to differently styled legs or exercise mismatches.

## BTC / India transfer qualifications
Use the book as historical conceptual context, not evidence of edge. Preserve chart-derived invalidation, explicit entry/exit alternatives, slippage/liquidity checks and separation of directional view from instrument payoff. Verify fresh contract metadata before options calculations: underlying identity, call/put, strike, precise expiry/timezone, quote and settlement currency, multiplier/lot, exercise/settlement style and tradable tick. BTC perpetual funding, mark/index/liquidation and nonlinear contract units are outside these stock examples. Indian lot sizes, expiry calendars and settlement obligations cannot be inferred from US stock pages. Contract data absent → calculations conditional or unavailable; no invented quantity, account value, risk tolerance or sizing authorization. No trade execution or live advice in this review.
