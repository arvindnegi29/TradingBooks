### Universe → regime → shortlist → trigger → execution
Ch5 distinguishes a system from its holding-period style. Most examples are daily US equity swing trades over several sessions/weeks; do not substitute a5minuteBTC signal and call it the same tested system.
Ch6watchlist price10-100USD,500kshares/day,beta>2 and50names is a historical US screen, not BTC/NSE requirements. Beta measures benchmark sensitivity, not guaranteed volatility or smooth trends. Underlying share activity does not prove option liquidity.
Ch7-8five states combine20/50MA order/slopes,separation and pullback behavior: strong/weak bullish,strong/weak bearish,range. Source chart baseline usesSMAs, while regime prose saysMA without complete thresholds. Strong/weak classification is partly subjective; disclose MA type,input,warmup,slope/separation/breach tolerances for any numerical adaptation. Preserve developing/uncertain transitions until actual confirmatory candles arrive. Touch/cross alone does not reproduce retrospective author answers.
Choose compatible setup family without forcing broad context to override missing instrument confirmation. An unclear broad market can still justify researching both sides, not an automatic trade.

### Ten source setups and important boundaries
Bullish: pullback,coiled spring,bullish divergence,blue-sky breakout,bullish base breakout. Bearish: relief rally,bearish divergence,gap-down,blue-sea breakdown,rising-wedge breakdown (Ch9-10).
Scans propose candidates; formal pattern rules, later quality preferences, entry triggers and risk tests are different stages. A scan endpoint comparison is not actual pivot divergence, line construction or a complete pattern test. There are documented scan/formal-rule inconsistencies; never copy a scan as a verified detector.
Divergence requires distinct causal price swings (source≥5trading-day spacing) and stated indicator version. Multiple oscillator agreements are correlated transformations, not independent votes. MACD12/26/9,RSI5,CCI20,OBV and stochastic5/3 or5/3/3 differ from Grimes'3/10SMA MACD, CarterTTM and existingRSI14 defaults. Do not silently change default tools or invent smoothing fidelity.
Coil/breakout/base/wedge rules use prior context,consolidation duration,closing-price boundaries,volume/OBV and sometimes anticipated entries before price breakout. OBV trendline break does not prove actual institutional holdings. A historical OBV/price chart cannot supply intrabar aggressor flow.
Gap-down is a completed US cash-session gap below preceding daily range with later trigger/invalidation sequence; it is not FVG or arbitrary BTCUTC candle gap. Intrabar trigger versus gap-fill invalidation requires ordered observations.
Bullish/bearish conditions are not perfect symmetric copies: preserve actual formal rules and classify conflicting prose/scan as unresolved. Source10%room-to-target/1.25RRminimum and US-dollar trigger buffers are historical preferences, not current budget/venue rules.

### Execution/risk gates
Ch11prior-session high/low entry with0.02/0.05USD buffers and last-two-US-hours preference require separate local validation. Close-defined setup cannot be known before final close; next-bar open may gap past trigger. Do not treat late-entry advocacy as permission to chase: recalculate present risk/reward, costs and invalidation or WAIT.
Stops described by8%price distance,structure,2ATR14 orSAR and targets1.5-2R/3ATR are alternative methods, not defaults or interchangeable after outcome. A price-percentage stop is not account-risk percentage. Size from the user's applicable cash budget, actual multiplier/lot/currency and costs; round down and NO TRADE if minimum size fails budget.
Source mental-stop/removal-overnight/option-alert-only policies do not establish continuous protection or a loss cap. Do not remove a user's protective plan, expand risk, add to invalidated losses, make automatic reversals or acquire new execution powers.
ATR is underlying price-unit movement, not option premium percentage. SAR can whipsaw in ranges. Matched risk/reward geometry and two/three technical reasons do not prove net expectancy.

### Options: quarantine printed errors; use correct structure math
Read carr-pdf246-301.md before using options concepts from this book: several written legs,breakevens and intrinsic-value definitions are wrong. Clearly label the corrected calculation as an independent correction, not a literal author rule.
For nonnegative settlement underlyingS, strikeK, per-unit entrycallc/putp, before costs:
- Longcall=max(S-K,0)-c; expiryBE=K+c; longput=max(K-S,0)-p; expiryBE=K-p.
- Shortcall=c-max(S-K,0); expiryBE=K+c, potentially unbounded loss ifS unbounded. Shortput=p-max(K-S,0); expiryBE=K-p, worst terminal lossK-p atS=0.
For matched same-expiry quantities withL<H,widthW:
|Structure|Correct legs|Maxgrossgain / maxgrossloss|ExpiryBE|
|---|---|---|---|
|Bullcall|BuyLcall,sellHcall;debitD|W-D / D|L+D|
|Bullput|SellHput,buyLput;creditC|C / W-C|H-C|
|Bearcall|SellLcall,buyHcall;creditC|C / W-C|L+C|
|Bearput|BuyHput,sellLput;debitD|W-D / D|H-D|
Multiply per-unit outcomes only by verified aligned contract multiplier and quantity. Include fees/spreads; expiry payoff bounds are not pre-expiry marks, available liquidity or protection against mismatched legs, early exercise/delivery/settlement obligations.
Long same-strike straddle payoff=abs(S-K)-(c+p); debitD=c+p gives expiryBEK±D where attainable. Both options are purchased. Legging is directional until opposite leg exists and becomes directional again after one leg exits. A correct directional move, longgamma or losing/winning-leg label does not guarantee aggregate profit after IVcrush,theta,totaldebit and spreads.
US100share multiplier,oldexpirycalendars/strikegrids/margin/assignment/IRA/borrow assumptions are not BTC/Indian rules. Actual option bid/ask,liquidity,expiry/settlement,IV/Greeks and supported repricing remain mandatory. Delta×underlyingmove is local approximation, not an executable premium target.
