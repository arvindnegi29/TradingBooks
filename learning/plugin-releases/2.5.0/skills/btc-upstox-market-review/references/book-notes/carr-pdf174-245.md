## Shared framing and definitions
PDF174–177 distinguish a setup from a guaranteed outcome, split five bullish/five bearish patterns, and defer execution/exit detail to Ch11. Candlestick vocabulary assumed: doji, hammer, piercing, morning/evening star and engulfing. A valid setup initially makes a shortlist; final filtering and execution remain separate. Market types are contextual preferences tied to Ch8, not automatically universal exclusion rules.

PDF178: uptrend = higher lows over at least three months; higher highs preferred but optional. Weak uptrend: mostly rising20MA, mostly above consistently rising50MA. Strong:20MA always rising and above consistently rising50MA; larger separation means stronger. PDF201 defines downtrend through lower highs over at least three months, lower lows optional; strong20MA consistently falling below falling50MA; weak20MA can rise/fall but mostly below consistently falling50MA. PDF214's relief-rally weak definition instead says20MA mostly falling below falling50MA; preserve this variation.

Scans are historical Stockcharts daily-close candidate generators, not complete rules. Unless separately stated, all-volume and price thresholds below are shares and US dollars, unsuitable for direct portability. SMA means simple moving average in scan language; prose often merely says MA.

## Five bullish setups (Ch9)

### Pullback — PDF177–183; printed158–164
Preferred market: strong/weak bullish. Uptrending stock pulls to rising20MA, between20/50MA, or50MA; rising support trendline confluence preferred. Slow stochastic(5,3) dips to/below20. Current bullish reversal candle (hammer/engulfing/piercing/doji) confirms shortlist signal; bearish/red candle means wait until another session. No exact price-entry or stop rule here: Ch11 dependency. QCOM illustration explicitly includes premature signal and stop-dependent survival, not flawless timing.

Main scan PDF179–180: SMA60(volume)>500000; SMA60(close)>15; current bullish engulfing (alternatively piercing/hammer/doji); SMA20(close)>SMA50(close); close<close[-5]*1.15. Author enlarges volume threshold to1m and multiplier1.20 for a large list; lowers multiplier1.10 or below for a small list. This multiplier advice is logically reversed for this '<' inequality: increasing upper bound admits more, not fewer, stocks. Do not silently fix.

Alternate PDF180–181: SMA20(volume)>1m; SMA60(close)>5; SMA20>SMA50>SMA200; SMA50>SMA50[-10]; SMA200>SMA200[-20]; close<close[-5]; close[-7]>close[-20]; SMA50<=close<=SMA20; open<=close; slow stochastic%K(5,3)>%D(5,3). It does not explicitly require oversold20, three-month higher lows or named candle: manual validation needed.

### Coiled spring — PDF183–189; printed164–170
Preferred strong/weak bullish or range-bound. Existing uptrend; at least three-month new high within last20 trading days; consolidating near high in narrowing sideways/slightly descending triangle/flag/pennant. Coil7–20 days, cannot angle upward. No part of coil reaches or exceeds50MA; crossing20MA allowed. Draw upper/lower boundaries; upper breakout is buy trigger, close below lower boundary invalidates. PDF188 permits wick excursions outside coil if candle bodies stay within it, which creates an operational distinction from its strict MA restriction. Initial play is described as price-pattern based; later preferences add indicators.

Scan PDF185–186: SMA60(volume)>500k; SMA60(close)>10; close>=open; high<=high[-5]; low>=low[-5]; SMA20>SMA50*1.08. Alternate: same60-day volume/price thresholds; SMA50<SMA20; ADX14<ADX14[-20]*0.41. Author explicitly demands visual elimination of false hits. Neither scan measures the full duration, narrowing or recent-high requirements.

### Bullish divergence — PDF189–194; printed170–175
Preferred range-bound, weak bullish, weak bearish. Long-range uptrend =50MA above rising200MA (50 may itself fall);50 crossing under200 invalidates. Price below50MA, at least two progressively lower clear price lows separated by >=5 trading days; latest low paired with higher low in >=2 of MACD, MACD histogram, stochastic, RSI, OBV, CCI at earlier book settings.200MA/support confluence preferred. Bullish reversal daily candle triggers signal. No local explicit stop/entry-price rule.

Scan PDF191–192: SMA60(volume)>500k; SMA60(close)>10; low[-1]<low[-20] and low[-1]<low[-3]; MACD histogram(12,26,9)>its[-15]; CCI20>its[-15]; RSI5>its[-15]; SMA50>SMA200; open<close. Endpoint comparisons are not pivot divergence tests; rising200 condition absent. Visual FWLT chart PDF193 labels RSI5, CCI20 and MACD12/26/9; BRCM PDF194 uses MACD histogram, stochastic and CCI.

### Blue sky breakout — PDF195–199; printed176–180
Preferred strong bullish. Close exceeds previous pivot closing high; pivot within preceding20 days and followed by5–20 bars selloff/sideways consolidation. New close is highest for >=3 months. New closing high /52-week intraday low <=3.0 (example wording instead says strictly below3x). OBV at >=3-month high; breakout candle close>open. All conditions same day. PDF198 explicitly draws resistance across closing prices, not wicks.

Scan PDF197: new52-week high; SMA20(volume)>500k; SMA60(close)>10; close<=minimum low260 days*3; OBV>OBV[-60]. Scan52-week high stricter than3-month setup, but OBV endpoint test weaker than maximum test; no pivot/consolidation/candle condition. PDF197 AAPL prose says at least three down days, unlike formal5-bar minimum. CREE prose PDF198 says prior pivot in late April and breakout April22 following16 days; its chart visibly shows April–May pivot/consolidation. Treat textual dates as inconsistent, not executable chronology.

### Bullish base breakout — PDF200–208; printed181–189
Preferred weak bullish, either bearish strength, range-bound. Downtrend followed by >=30 trading-day base: flat/falling rectangles or symmetric/ascending/descending triangles; rising wedge explicitly excluded. MACD forms higher lows during base; OBV crosses above line drawn over its highs. Buy signal first close>open candle after OBV break, including break day if bullish. Price breakout is not prerequisite; final preferences favor still inside base. Drawings PDF201–202 confirm permitted shapes. AKS PDF205 has price breakout before OBV trigger; KLAC PDF206 has OBV trigger before price breakout; JNPR PDF207 falling rectangle and improving MACD show timing can vary.

Scan PDF203–204: SMA20(volume)>500k; SMA60(close)>10; SMA50>SMA20; SMA50<SMA50[-40]; maximum range30>range15>range5; OBV>OBV[-40]; MACD line12/26/9>its[-40]; SMA50<SMA200. 'Maximum range' is not rigorously defined here; do not substitute ATR or highest-high minus lowest-low without disclosure. Screen admits false hits and often catches OBV trigger late.

Five-period stochastic reaches/exceeds80; bearish reversal candle (doji/engulfing/gravestone/hanging man/evening star etc.) confirms. Positive candle means wait. ### Bearish divergence — PDF217–222; printed198–203
Preferred range-bound, weak bearish, weak bullish.50MA below falling200MA (50 itself can rise); sharp rally above50MA, >=2 higher clear price highs separated by >=5 trading days; latest paired with lower high in >=2 of MACD, histogram, stochastic, OBV, CCI, RSI. Resistance/200MA confluence favored. Bearish daily reversal candle signals.

### Gap down — PDF222–227; printed203–208
Preferred bullish strong/weak or range-bound, contrarian to stock uptrend. Narrative requires significant >=3-month prior high, though numbered conditions omit it. Steady uptrend:50MA steadily rising without major dips; price above50MA >=40 sessions. On completed gap day high<previous session low. Short trigger only subsequently when price trades below gap-day low; if price enters gap before trigger, invalidate. Gap-down open beyond trigger illustrated EBAY PDF225–226; cannot assume fill exactly at prior low. SWIR PDF225–226 confirms later trigger can follow several consolidation days. Scan PDF223–224: breakaway gap down; SMA60(volume)>500k; SMA60(close)>10; SMA20>SMA50*1.1. This proxy does not ensure steady50MA,40 sessions, or prior3-month high. Continuous BTC bars are not equivalent to cash-session opening gaps.

### Blue sea breakdown — PDF227–232; printed208–213
Preferred strong bearish. New closing low after prior new low within20 days; close lowest >=3 months;52-week high/current close <=2.0 (example says strictly greater than half high). OBV lowest >=3 months; close<open. Same-day full conditions. Unlike blue sky formal text, no explicit5-bar minimum in this passage; do not invent symmetry. No MA/trendline required (PDF230).

Scan PDF229: new52-week low; SMA20(volume)>500k; SMA60(close)>5; close>=maximum high260 days*0.5; OBV<OBV[-60]. Same scan/formal differences as blue sky. ### Rising wedge breakdown — PDF232–238; printed213–219
Preferred bullish weak/strong or range-bound. Uptrend narrowing into wedge; >=3 intraday touches of each boundary (not necessarily pivots), support rising faster than resistance. Flat-top ascending triangle allowed alongside both-lines-rising wedge. MACD lower highs during wedge; OBV below support line drawn under its lows. Short signal first close<open candle after OBV break, including break day if negative; price need not have exited wedge. NVDA PDF236 and CSCO PDF237 explicitly trigger before price boundary break, with source suggesting close-of-trigger-day entry. Cannot infer final indicator close in advance.

Scan PDF234–235: SMA20(volume)>500k; SMA60(close)>10; SMA50>SMA20; SMA50>SMA50[-40]; maximum range30>range15>range5; OBV<OBV[-40]; MACD line12/26/9<its[-40]; SMA50>SMA200. SMA50>SMA20 is surprising for uptrend proxy but must not silently reverse. Many false hits; often OBV signal already passed.

## Final shortlist filters — PDF239–245; printed220–226
These are stated as preferences, not all new mandatory detector conditions. Review >=6 months of daily chart plus weekly chart. Common historical preference: earnings >=2 weeks after entry. Relevant volume preferences: below-average pullback/relief rally/coil volume; above-average terminal divergence low/high, breakout/breakdown, gap or OBV signal day volume.

Pullback: no close below50MA, supporting line intact, no bearish indicator divergence/double top, no major weekly overhead MA. Coil: >7 and <=15 candles favored (formal validity7–20); no close under50MA; more positive than negative candles; >=2 of RSI/CCI/OBV rising; no weekly overhead MA. This later indicator preference supplements earlier price-only description. Bull divergence: strong >=2 divergences with >2 favored; support confluence; last low >10% away from20MA; no weekly overhead MA. Blue sky: >=6-month new high and repeated failed breakouts during consolidation favored. Bull base: longer base, more positive candles, >=2 RSI/CCI/OBV rising, still inside base, base near weekly20/50/200MA.

Relief: no close above50MA (text accidentally says 'dip'), containing downtrend line intact, no bullish divergence/double bottom/inverse head-and-shoulders. Bear divergence: strong multiple divergences, resistance confluence, last high >10% away20MA. Relief and bearish-divergence weekly criteria literally say not just UNDER major weekly MA, same as bullish text; probable copy error, not license to flip silently. Gap: pre-gap high at resistance and bearish divergence favored. Blue sea: >=6-month low and failed breakdown attempts favored. Wedge: still inside price boundaries, both-lines-rising favored over flat-top, more negative candles, >=2 RSI/CCI/OBV falling, weekly-MA confluence.

PDF244–245 expected percentage gain divided by initial percentage stop distance: source accepts >=1.25 if trailing stop, prefers>=2;10% gain /8% stop example. Reject candidates lacking >=10% potential before significant resistance. Exact stops and execution depend Ch11, not supplied here. These historical10% and1.25 rules should not automatically override current plugin risk governance. Source explicitly permits standing aside after extensive screening.

## Causal and transfer requirements for any later implementation
Freeze timeframe, completed-bar timestamp, session calendar, timezone, MA/indicator formula and adjustment policy. All daily-close conditions require completed candles; if entered on next bar model actual open/gap/slippage. Same-close fills need realistic market-on-close decision timing and must not use unknowable final close/OBV. Intrabar breakout events require ordered data to distinguish trigger versus invalidation first. Treat chart lines and pivot recognition as information available at that time; no hindsight-extrema selection, future-confirmed pivots backdated to lows/highs or line refits after outcome. Divergence spacing is between price extrema, not arbitrary endpoint differences. Overlapping MACD/histogram confirmations are correlated, not independent evidence.

Scans merely propose candidates; formal setup, quality preferences, final risk test and execution are separate stages. Record unresolved subjective terms (clear low/high, near, mostly, steadily, shrinking, major dips, average volume, support resistance, line-touch tolerance) explicitly rather than claiming exact book detector fidelity. Patterns do not establish expected return or win probability.

BTC:24/7 bars, daily boundary choices, spot/perpetual differences, exchange-local volume/OBV, funding, contract sizing, leverage/liquidation, fee/slippage and venue outage gaps differ from US stocks. Gap-down transfer particularly weak without genuine session gap. India:cash calendar/overnight gaps, corporate actions/adjustments, share-volume/rupee not dollar thresholds, instrument liquidity and current product-specific shorting/derivatives/settlement rules require independent verification. Index and derivative volume is not automatically cash-stock accumulation; option-chain volume/OI cannot substitute directly for underlying OBV. Earnings filter applies to individual equities, not directly to BTC or index; replace only through separately justified event-risk process. Historical parameters and chart anecdotes require forward-causal, out-of-sample validation before use as ranked detectors; reading establishes no transferable edge.
