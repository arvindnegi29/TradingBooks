## How to apply this book in a signal review
1. Name the trading timeframe, higher/lower context and proposed family: continuation, termination, level holding or level failing. These overlap and are not independent votes. A trend ending usually permits a range before any opposite trend. Preserve uncertain/transition states.
2. Separate context, candidate setup, trigger, invalidation, management and evidence of edge. A visual pattern, good reward/risk geometry, discipline or risk sizing does not itself prove positive net expectancy.
3. Use observable swing, momentum, rejection/acceptance and follow-through; keep participant-intent/accumulation/trapped-position narratives explicitly inferential. A breach is not automatically a sweep, a failed breakout or a new trend.
4. Retrieve only relevant reviewed passages. Give book/chapter/PDF pages, actual calculation/observation, contrary interpretation and implication. Do not treat all pages as a mandatory consensus engine.

### Causal structure and invalidation
First-order pivots require a following bar, higher orders require later confirmed pivots (Ch1 PDF32-35). Record pivot location AND first known-at time; define equality, outside bars and nonalternation. Historical labels cannot be used before confirmation. Keep source pivot orders separate from the plugin's existing STH/ITH and SMC definitions.
First broken HL/LH, trend-line breach or divergence is a warning; it may be a complex pullback rather than reversal. Evaluate opposing momentum, next structure, timeframe and acceptance before changing thesis (Ch3-5).
The same OHLC bar has many possible intrabar paths (PDF39-40). Do not invent order flow, high/low sequence or exact simultaneous stop/target ordering. Use finer actual data or declared conservative research assumptions.
Close-based failure/reclaim/hook and higher-timeframe evidence must be completed and available at decision time; no backward-filled final daily/weekly values, centered windows, future extrema or retrospectively selected ranges.

### Context-specific templates, not implemented detectors
Ch6 reviews: failure test; trend pullback at support/resistance; lower-timeframe breakout into pullback; complex pullback; Anti; prebreakout base; first pullback after breakout; failed breakout.
Failure test needs excursion beyond a significant level then decisive rejection, not every wick. Anti needs an old-trend warning, strong opposing impulse, then its FIRST controlled retracement; not every oscillator disagreement. Rising lows toward resistance and tight edge consolidation can pressure breakout rather than strengthen rejection. A price recross alone may be a normal retest.
Terms such as strong/reluctant, mature, meaningful, immediate, zone tolerance, climax and controlled pullback remain partly discretionary. Any numerical version must disclose its own frozen parameters/confirmation times and be labeled derived research, not a source-proven detector.
A selected example can justify analysis of existing exposure without supporting a fresh entry or add. Reentry needs a newly valid setup and remaining campaign budget; never rewrite a previous stop after seeing later recovery.

### Indicators and corroboration
Grimes modified MACD is SMA3(close)-SMA10(close); signal=SMA16(fast), no histogram (AppendixB PDF433-435). It differs from ordinary EMA MACD, Carter's proprietary TTM and RSI. State exact construction/seed/warmup/bar conventions for any computed proxy; standard MACD12/26/9 remains a different indicator. With trailing complete windows, fast first appears at observation10, signal25 (explicit implementation convention, not source validation).
Repeated hooks and divergence may arise from rolling-window outliers/gaps, trend duration or deceleration. Divergence needs distinct swings; no retracement means no valid swing-pair comparison. MACD is price-derived, not leading causal information, and is not independent of MA slope/crossovers.
Keltner2.25ATR historical channel table describes total bar-range containment, not confidence of future closes or probability of a winning trade (PDF215). Random-data rows look similar. Specify actual center/ATR period/smoothing; don't imply a complete numerical source specification when absent. Bands are alerts, not barriers or automatic fades.
Neighbor timeframe factor3-5 and square-root horizon scaling are heuristics, not laws. Honor explicitly requested timeframes. Relative strength means cross-market relative performance, not RSI; use compatible return basis, benchmark, sessions and timestamps. No blanket higher-timeframe-veto rule: an HTF range can contain a clean tradable LTF trend.

### Risk, accounting and execution
Fit size to defensible invalidation under the user's applicable cash budget, actual multiplier/currency/lot and costs. Do not move an invalid stop to fit desired quantity or import author1%/3% defaults, toy Kelly output or profit goals.
Keep initial R fixed for normalized performance. Distinguish net cash P&L, marked-to-market equity, cumulative closed-trade P&L, R multiple, current open risk and stressed/gap loss. Scale-ins, partials, refills and multiple stops need quantity-aware combined accounting. Unrealized profit or an entry-price stop is not risk-free.
Stop-market prioritizes exit without price certainty; stop-limit/marketable limit can remain partly/unfilled; touch is not a limit fill. Same-bar order, spread, adverse selection, missed winners, latency and gaps matter. Repeated attempts share a campaign budget. Correlated simultaneous exposures do not have the same drawdown math as sequential shrinking-equity losses.
Actual portfolio/account exposure cannot be inferred through read-only market tools. Use explicitly supplied trade records/budgets or label a hypothetical; no balances, holdings, private fills or execution are authorized.

### Evidence and research integrity
Expectancy must be net of actual costs and include uncertainty/tails. Historical high hit rate, RR, normalized P&L or a significant in-sample test is insufficient for current edge.
Chapter10 examples are teaching-selected and partly untraded (PDF309); do not aggregate into performance. AppendixC68trades are educational, lack executable details and contain rounded derived fields. They are not BTC/NSE training/validation data or a strategy track record.
The book calls R multiples %R; don't accidentally multiply/divide100. Preserve both dollar/net-equity and R views; normalization does not repair harmful sizing. Closed-trade curves omit open-equity drawdowns. Lag control-chart reference mean/SD so today's point cannot dilute its own flag. Control bands are diagnostic, not normally distributed probability bounds.
P-values are conditional tail probabilities under model assumptions, not probabilities the trader has/no edge. Displayed0.000 is rounded, not zero. Address dependence, nonstationarity, tails, small subgroups, multiple testing/selection, survivorship and chronological holdouts. Keep classifications and rule versions fixed at decision time; compare discretionary overlays against the unchanged baseline.
Psychological typologies, learning timelines, clinical/neuroscience claims and risky exercises are not adopted. A compliant loss can coexist with absent edge; don't blame the user's emotions for a strategy/data defect. Paper/replay study remains useful.

## Source audit: independent corrections
These are source-quality notes, not a silently corrected return series:
- PrefacePDF14 misdirects risk references to6/10; actual management/risk are8/9. AppendixCPDF442 saysChapter13, but the statistical analysis isChapter12; there are12chapters.
- PDF193 higher-high wording conflicts with higher-low pressure inFig6.18/PDF194.
- PDF272 buy1@10,buy1@15,sell1@20 is mislabeled FIFO: FIFO realizes10/leaves15cost; LIFO realizes5/leaves10; at common mark total P&L agrees.
- PDF283 labels (1-f)^t compound loss; it is retained-equity factor. Loss=1-(1-f)^t. Recovery from drawdown d is d/(1-d), before other changes/costs.
- PDF29025k/2k=12.5, not11.5. PDF289 conflates percentage increase in profit with terminal wealth.
- AppendixB's universal same-length EMA-closer-than-SMA claim is not valid in a steady arithmetic ramp for alpha2/(n+1): both lag(n-1)/2. SMA20 nulls sinusoid periods20/k, not every multiple k*20. Fast SMA3-SMA10 on unit ramp=3.5, a smoothed slope measure; change of fast reflects acceleration, not its positive level alone.
- Glossary TR,CLT and regression explanations are simplified: use TR=max(H-L,abs(H-prevClose),abs(L-prevClose)); standardized means/sums approach normal under assumptions, not the raw histogram of die outcomes; association is not causation.
