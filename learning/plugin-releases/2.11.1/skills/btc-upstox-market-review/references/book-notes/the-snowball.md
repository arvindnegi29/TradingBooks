## Limits for BTC and Indian-market reviews

- Do not route chart patterns, support/resistance, options selection or timing through this biography.
- “Margin of safety” is not a numeric price buffer, stop or sizing formula for BTCUSD, NIFTY, options or futures.
- Separate long-term business ownership from leveraged, mark-to-market trading. Buffett's results, capital access and horizon do not compare directly to a retail derivatives trade.
- Keep fresh market data and technical evidence primary. Add a brief Buffett lens only when relevant and state whether it supports, challenges, is inconclusive or not applicable.
- Do not count anecdotes as independent confirmations; preserve counterexamples and failures.
- No profitability, causal edge, live price or trade advice is established by this book.
