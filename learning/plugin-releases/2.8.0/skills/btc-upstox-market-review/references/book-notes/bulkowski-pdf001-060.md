## Source rules and marking consequences
- Ch1PDF19–27: identify aligned peaks/valleys, then horizontal boundaries, diagonals and curves. Pattern labels come after observed structure, not before. Same price action can nest or support different names; describe overlap once rather than count independent confirmations. Early chapter explicitly relaxes inbound-trend context for eye training; formal later pattern chapters determine eligibility. Exhibit1.4 rejects a loose cup-like shape as a strict cup; silhouette alone is insufficient.
- Ch2PDF29–33: source minorhigh/low uses roughly5days on each side, with3–5day/pattern-dependent and visual exceptions. This is not a complete deterministic rule. In a5left/5right numerical adaptation a pivot is usable only after the fifth right bar closes; record both pivot and known-at time, equality/tie policy and separation. Do not silently turn5daily sessions into5BTC5minute bars and claim source equivalence. Two-day local turns can be insignificant; discretion must be labeled rather than passed off as a scan result.
- Ch3PDF35–44: use valley anchors for an uptrend support line; peak anchors for downtrend resistance. Two touches minimum, three preferred. Source allows approximate touches, internal(price-cutting), external(extrema-hugging) and curved lines; prefers external for his stop reasoning but does not ban internal lines. Label which fit was used, count eligible touches and retain endpoint/version history. A pierce or close across is a warning, not guaranteed reversal. Wider-spaced, longer and shallower lines have favorable historical claims; screen-angle is not scale-invariant and no causal edge is established by those claims.
- Source logscale can reveal large historical proportional moves; arithmetic ruler distances should not be read as dollar heights on logscale. Store actual price anchors and axis type; calculate target arithmetic numerically. A straight line in logprice is generally curved in arithmeticprice. Do not compute future boundary levels by applying a linear slope to log-coordinate anchors.
- Ch4PDF45–53: support/resistance are bands, can exchange role, and can fail. Mark HCRs(horizontal consolidation regions) with temporal start/end and price band; dense/long/tight overlap preferred over a wandering region. Carry forward as potential obstacles or retest context, not permanent floors/ceilings. Prior peaks, valleys, trendlines, whole patterns and round numbers may matter; keep nearest meaningful levels and avoid filling chart with every possible number.
- PDF47–48: historical gapSAR20%support/25%resistance and HCR41–55%are source results without reproduced sample definition here; not live probabilities or BTC/NSE calibration. Narrative causes involving fear, greed, value buyers and stop clusters are inference, not directly observed orderflow.
- PDF49–51: source avoids round-number stops/targets with historical USstock price buffers. Adapt to verified tick and structural invalidation rather than always shifting a stop to end in a particular digit; no avoidance rule guarantees absence of stop running. Round-number interest is conditional, not measured resting orders.
- Ch5PDF55–60: true two-bar price gap is currentlow>priorhigh or currenthigh<priorlow. This differs from SMCthree-bar FVG, openinggap(close-to-open) and missing-bar/feed problems. Mark both exact price edges and creationtime; classify data issue before market interpretation.
- Four gap categories: area/common in congestion with quick curl/fill; breakaway leaving congestion and sustaining trend; continuation/measuring during an established move with follow-through; exhaustion near trend end followed by overlap/consolidation/reversal. Final type often depends on future bars: provisional at creation, updated when evidence arrives. No retrospective exhaustion label available at first gap.
- Source says continuation gaps rare and intraday gaps mostly low volume; these are source observations for its equity context, not universal BTCdata laws. Missing exchange candles do not become tradable gaps. Contemporary volume and session evidence must be verified separately.
- Continuation gap target uses midpointG and trendstartA: upward projectionG+(G-A); downwardG-(A-G). PDF60example21.57+(21.57-18.95)=24.19 is correct. It is a conditional measured-move hypothesis, not guaranteed target or timing. Dates/averaged closure days are daily-equity statistics, not intradaybar counts.

## Source inconsistencies and limitations
- Ch4quizPDF53 answer1saysG(whole numbers)notusuallySAR, contradicting its own round-number sectionPDF48–51. Preserve as an unresolved answer-key inconsistency; do not adopt answer as fact.
- Ch5PDF57table says exhaustion usually closes within a week while surrounding historical averages9/14days are longer; definitions/average statistics and calendar/tradingday conventions are not interchangeable. Do not turn either into a deterministic timeout.
- Claim that chart patterns reveal smart-money footprints is source interpretation, not institutional identification. No participant positions can be recovered from these historical daily charts.
- Phrases about prediction, superior pattern size, volume effect or early retirement are not independently reproduced results or current performance promises. Author says method visual, not mechanical; code implementation must disclose numerical research assumptions.
