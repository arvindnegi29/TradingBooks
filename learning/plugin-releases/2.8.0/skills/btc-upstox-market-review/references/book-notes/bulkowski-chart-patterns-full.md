## Required application
Read [chart-pattern workflow](../chart-pattern-workflow.md) for actual annotation/scan process,[coverage registry](../pattern-coverage.json) for operational status,and [original geometry schematics](../../assets/chart-pattern-reference.svg) for teaching. Source anchors and rules belong in detailed notes; workflow adds explicitly labeled local conventions. Reference retrieval is not a claim of automated scanning. Geometry helper only computes supplied-anchor lines/projections and is separately arithmetic-tested.

Core distinctions:
1. Price swings precede shape labels. Record inboundtrend,morphology,confirmation,execution and outcome separately. A confirmed losing pattern remains historically confirmed; an attractive unconfirmed shape is only a candidate.
2. Genuine swing contacts,interior traversals,proportions and context matter. Two fitted lines alone can enclose a false triangle. Source accepts near-touch/internal-line and pivot exceptions; document borderline alternatives rather than fabricating a unique solution.
3. Minorhigh/low5day lookaround implies future-confirmation lag in a numerical adaptation. No backdated signal before known-at time; no reanchoring historical boundaries with future bars. Source daily durations cannot become5minute bar counts without separate research assumptions.
4. Support/resistance are bands that can fail and exchange role. HCRcongestion,touch reference,breakout threshold,wholepatternhigh/low,projection and trade stop are separate objects. Avoid exact-price claims from lowresolution screenshots.
5. Close confirms most textbook breakouts. Historical intrabar buy-stop examples are a different entry policy,not license to use a confirming close before it happened. Nextopen/fill can gap. Do not silently impose one book's trigger policy on Volman's distinct model.
6. H&S bottom risingneckline uses RIGHTarmpitHIGH; H&Stop fallingneckline uses RIGHTarmpitLOW. Neckline measured height uses its value at headtime,then projects from actualbreakout. Chart lettering sometimes repeats; name semantic roles.
7. Bulkowski throwback=return afterupbreak,pullback=return afterdownbreak,within30calendardays in source definition. Generic pullbacks in other authors differ. Retest is not automatically failure.
8. Strictbust needs confirmedoriginalbreak,limitedinitialexcursion and close beyond opposite WHOLEpattern extreme. Early adverse neckline/shoulder/trendline break is not always fullbust. Countbusts_so_far,not eventualsinglebust known only later.
9. Apex is a TIME alert for possible minorhigh OR low; it is not a price target or guaranteed bearish turn. Swingrule is a PRICE projection with no promised timing:2*oldextreme−retracementextreme. Flags use poleheight from the specified prebreakextreme; different bases must not be mixed.
10. Allvisualappendix families are catalogued in PDF301–344notes. Broadening,wedge,cup,diamond,horn,pipe,island,scallop,etc silhouettes do not provide the missing duration/tolerance/trigger/risk specifications. Mark recognition_only or notimplemented; do not invent a complete source rule.

## Historical statistics: preserve what was actually measured
PDF203 warns subsequent figures are bull-market/perfect-trade examples,not actual expected results. Directionfrequency,targetattainment,retestfrequency,ultimatehigh return,net trade winrate and portfolioCAGR are different metrics. A triangle breakout around70–75%of time toapex is not75%tradeconfidence. An83%thresholdhit or80%within±5%of swingtarget is not a forecast of this trade's net success.

Study outcomes are mostly USdaily/weeklyequities; some monthly setups have longholds. Record marketregime,period,N,target/returndefinition and costs when available. Singlebust,no-throwback,ultimatehigh and acceptedfinalpattern subgroups are retrospective; avoid selecting them as available livefilters. Overlapping patterns/timeframes/volume are correlated observations,not independent probabilities. Small samples and missing testdefinitions remain explicit.

Ch29PDF321–322 reports particular historical in/out-of-sample results with average254day out-of-samplehold and maximum72%loss. Average25%P/L is not25%CAGR. Above/below200SMAcomparisons do not establish a universal MAfilter. The fixedpenny/belowwholepatternstop and no-trail policy are not plugin defaults; do not repeat them for a user budget or imply stops cap losses.
