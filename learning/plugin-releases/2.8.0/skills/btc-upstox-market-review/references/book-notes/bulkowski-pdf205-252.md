## Source inconsistencies and implementation cautions

1. PDF216 prose discusses an ascending-triangle alternate-stop comparison as baseline6%,74% and maximum33%, but its table gives ascending4%,70%,38%;6%,74%,33% are descending-triangle figures. Likely pattern-name/table-reference error. Do not silently ingest prose as ascending baseline.
2. PDF238 table visibly says time-exit$1.05 or1.1%; on$10,000, $1.05 is0.0105%. Narrative confirms$1.05, so percent appears erroneous. Never use1.1% in calculations without correction note.
3. PDF237 says next open12.62 is more than5% above target12.58; it is about0.32% above target and about5.34% above11.98 entry. Interpret as >5% above entry, flag wording error.
4. PDF245–248 alternates less-than10%,10%-or-less, at-least10%, and more-than10% for bust qualification/count termination. Exact equality is unresolved. A reproducible scanner must define strict/non-strict comparison, rounding, price reference and whether excursion is intraday high/low versus close. Use source-specific language and an ambiguity flag rather than false precision.
5. PDF249 ALB text places buying at open at B while stating B closes above top. Close-known-at-open would be lookahead if same candle. Enforce confirm close first, next-bar open; original example label alone insufficient.
6. PDF210 inference that16% head-stop hits means maximum84% winners ignores trades neither stopped nor profitably closed and depends on horizon/exit. Use as stop-hit complement only, not a complete win-rate model.
7. PDF214 says all examples used basic post-breakout buy and below-whole-pattern stop, but examples explicitly include early triple-bottom entry, nearer minor-low/RS stops, delayed retracement entries and no initial stop. Treat as broad summary, not literal per-example compliance.
8. PDF236 intraday tall bar rule suggests selling near high or raising to middle; high is unknowable until bar complete and a guessed intraday high is not executable precision. Tall2–3x20bar mean and Bollinger squeeze lack exact parameter definitions. State conditional heuristic and separate it from close-based tests.
9. Historical perfect-trade averages, example returns, and simulation average P/L measure different quantities. Source sometimes uses causal language (“throwback hurts performance,” small patterns do worse, high volume makes throwback likelier). These are observational associations, not independent causal proof.

## Suggested marking/scanning contract derived from evaluation

Record chart timeframe, price-adjustment basis and visible data limits; mark anchor dates/prices with names (head low, neckline-at-head, actual breakout, whole-pattern highest high, throwback low), not just letters. Show pattern interior and pretrend context. State candidate/confirmed/busted status and which close completed it. Keep order level, actual fill, structural invalidation, stop and projection separate. For wide shapes provide both full-pattern structural stop and explicitly labeled nearer tactical alternative; calculate loss distance rather than hide it. Use a penny only as historical US-stock example buffer; tick-size/volatility adaptation is an implementation choice, not source rule. Flag gaps and possible slippage. Mark external resistance and alternative interpretations. Projection must not silently become guaranteed target or exit instruction. Do not label future single/double/triple bust outcome as known at entry. No live market call, plugin edit or backtest was performed in this review.
