# Comprehensive entry-scan coverage

An exhaustive vocabulary list is not a trading model. Select operational definitions, preserve them between reviews, calculate what the data supports, and classify every requested item. The six experimental rules in the backtest engine are only six price-based operationalizations, not all of the concepts below.

## Required observations

Use source-backed completed Daily/4H context, 1H intermediate structure, 30m/15m setups, and 5m execution when that is the requested scope. Fetch warmup for each indicator, preserve actual candle-open and close times and use no future-confirmed swing at an earlier date. For live reviews, refresh executable quotes/depth independently. Historical ledger performance cannot confirm today's entry.

## Definitions and classification

- **STH/STL and ITH/ITL:** choose explicit pivot widths and hierarchy rules. A 2-left/2-right swing becomes known at the second right candle's close. An intermediate swing hierarchy needs its own fixed rule; do not silently label every short-term pivot an ITH. Keep protected high/low state and the time of confirmation.
- **BOS, MSS, CHOCH:** state external versus internal structure, protected swings and required close/displacement. Distinguish trend continuation from an opposing first break and a confirmed shift. A wick through a level is not a close-confirmed break.
- **Internal/external liquidity:** define the current dealing range, external endpoints and internal confirmed highs/lows or equal-high/low tolerance. These are price-based liquidity hypotheses; absent order data cannot prove resting stop inventory.
- **FVG:** strict three-candle high/low non-overlap with boundaries, direction, creation close time and mitigation/invalidation state. **IFVG:** a close invalidates the original gap and a later opposite-side retest confirms the entry model; identify both times.
- **Basic OB:** define the last opposing candle before qualifying displacement and a meaningful structure break; specify body versus wick boundaries. **Advanced OB:** add separately defined sweep/imbalance/refinement and mitigation requirements. Do not relabel the basic proxy advanced.
- **Mitigation and breaker blocks:** define the structural failure/break sequence, block origin, invalidation and later retest separately. Do not label every OB retest a distinct mitigation-block pattern. Unsupported lifecycle rules remain not implemented.
- **Sweep/reversal:** take a predefined liquidity reference, reclaim it and require the specified lower-timeframe confirmation. **Liquidity continuation:** take it with close/displacement and sustained acceptance/failed retest in the breakout direction. Show the actual sequence and cancellation rule.
- **Inducement/fakeout:** define the internal reference and its chronological relationship to the higher-timeframe point of interest. A post-hoc motive is not a detection. A failed breakout needs a fixed breakout and reclaim condition.
- **CRT/Turtle Soup:** choose reference candle/session/range, sweep, reclaim and confirmation; identify the range before the sweep. **ICT/ITC:** distinguish the user's intended acronym/model if material; do not imply one universal model. Premium/discount needs a stated dealing range.
- **Chart patterns and price action:** read [Chart pattern workflow](chart-pattern-workflow.md) and [coverage registry](pattern-coverage.json). Choose a finite named pattern with tolerance, pivot confirmations, interior traversal, inbound-trend role, neckline/boundary and trigger policy. Record visual assessment versus computed detector separately; mark developing, confirmed, invalidated, adverse break or strict bust with exact known-at times. Use book-specific H&S right-armpit slope overrides and keep apex-time alerts separate from price targets. Glossary-only patterns lack full operational rules. Do not say every chart pattern was scanned if no detector ran. Use the arithmetic helper only for supplied-anchor levels, never as detection proof.
- **EMA20/50, RSI14, ATR14, volume:** calculate actual series with disclosed seed/smoothing/warmup. Relative volume requires a session-comparable baseline; spot-index volume cannot be fabricated. These contextual indicators do not create independent statistical confirmations when redundant.
- **Order flow, OI, funding:** true CVD/footprint/absorption requires a sufficiently complete timestamped trade series and aggressor convention. A short public-print sample or displayed book is a limited snapshot, not historical swing accumulation. OI cannot identify the direction of new positions. Historical tests using these filters require aligned historical features; otherwise mark filter validation unavailable.

Every detected candidate should carry concept/model, venue/contract, timeframe, exact boundaries, relevant event/confirmation timestamps, source observations, entry trigger, structural invalidation, target convention, expected costs and opposing evidence. Detecting a pattern is separate from triggering it, validating historical expectancy, and having sufficient fresh execution evidence. Show WAIT where any critical execution condition is missing.

For overlapping concepts in one candidate, describe the sequence once. Do not sum correlated labels into a claimed confidence percentage. A confluence score remains an illustrative decision aid until tested against an independent sample and probability-calibrated.
