## Chapter1 (15-27): risk, gaps and failed highs
Predeclare invalidation, order semantics, quantity and exit rules. Do not replace risk control with recovery targets, opinion defense, emotional reversal or doubled size. Candles cannot prove who was liquidated or institutional intent.
TASR example18-23:2,000 shares at27.80, intended26.20 stop-limit/32.60 target means3,200 risk and9,600 reward before costs. Opening23.78 implies8,040 loss, not3,200. Exit14.02 loses27,560. Subsequent4,000-share short14.04 to20.83 loses27,160. Starting100,000 ends45,280, requiring120.85% recovery. Five independent source-arithmetic assertions checked planned loss, opening-gap loss, ending equity and approximate bond/ES equivalence; these are not strategy tests.
Triggered stop-limit can remain unfilled if prices violate its limit; source does not provide both stop/limit fields separately. Stop-market can execute past stop. Neither guarantees a maximum cash loss. Indian session gaps and24/7 BTC jumps/outages both matter. Do not tighten an invalid structural stop to fit a minimum lot.
Failed-high stock swing hypothesis25-27: new52-week high, RSI7 lower peak, lower volume than prior thrust, daily close below previous52-week high, next-session-open short. Example buffer0.25 above33.45 extreme. Source exit shifts from daily reversal to60-minute reversal after discretionary support break. Explicit anchor/support/confirmation rules needed; detector not implemented. Do not choose eventual low with future knowledge or treat a daily stock rule as5-minute BTC sweep. Selected winning examples are not win-rate evidence.
## Chapter2 (28-41): consistent process
Separate setup execution/management rules from allocation/cash-risk rules. Stop distance and contract count cannot be universal. Before entry, size falls as structural distance rises under same budget; after entry, do not widen stop to rescue a loss.
Carter records25-trade blocks and impulse deviations30-31. Use as an audit unit, not statistical proof. Keep all consecutive eligible trades, net costs, rule version, causal timestamps, exit reason and compliance tags. One valid losing trade does not imply bad process; one winning impulse trade does not validate it. Preserve Chan's holdout/multiple-testing controls rather than monthly selecting winners on the same data.
Avoid reactive indicator changes after1-2 losses, chasing missed entries and size escalation after euphoria or to fund purchases. Do not infer the user's psychology or repeat author percentages as calibrated probabilities.
Despite its heading, paper-trading discussion37 acknowledges platform learning and forward testing; emotional/fill limitations do not justify forcing live losses.
Plateau2.5-times growth/half withdrawal38-39 is a historical illustration, not an attainable-return promise or default capital plan. Reserve funds do not justify wiping a trading stake. Author's two-stopout daily pause40 is an optional hypothesis, not a universal validated breaker. Do not adopt punitive cold-water/financial penalties, psychological diagnoses or personal anecdotes as scientific advice.
## Chapter3 (42-55): infrastructure and information
Hardware/software prices, Windows7/Security Essentials/Intel-Mac instructions and broker ownership are historical2011-12, not current recommendations. Do not modify the user's computer, delete cookies, reinstall or reconnect based on a book passage. Preserve working authentication.
Transferable ideas: reliable timestamped quotes; known chart/execution venue mapping; backup data, power, connectivity and failure plan; focused evidence rather than endless screens.
Order-entry examples48-54 discuss bracket/OCO roles, partial exit quantities and accidental wrong-side orders. They do not authorize plugin execution. A stop moved to entry is not guaranteed net breakeven through costs/gaps. The author's dismissal of LevelII is opinion: depth may help, but displayed liquidity can cancel and does not prove intent.
Newsletter/TV conviction54-55 is not probability evidence. Neither automatically fade news nor disable scheduled-event checks.
## Chapter4 (56-77): product units and historical specifications
Verify tick, multiplier, currency, expiry, settlement, notional, actual liquidity and costs for the current contract. Margin is not economic risk. Same contract count or point stop across products does not mean equal risk. Historical US tax/PDT, margin, hours, fees and delivery claims cannot become current NSE/Delta rules. Futures trading almost24hours does not imply24/7 or guaranteed fill.
Continuous futures charts are not executable contracts; rolls can create artificial gaps62-63. Distinguish roll basis from perpetual funding. ES/YM approximate price co-movement is not a constant hedge ratio; fractional tick granularity alone does not prove better realized execution.
