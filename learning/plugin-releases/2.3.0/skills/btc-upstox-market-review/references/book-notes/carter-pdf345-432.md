## Chapter18, Box plays, PDF345–365
- Horizontal consolidation with two tests of each boundary. Tests must alternate in meaningful high-low structure; three clustered highs plus one low are not enough (PDF361–362). After fourth test, price must retreat into box about25% of its width before setup complete (PDF346,354,356).
- Entry buy-stop one instrument tick above top and sell-stop one tick below bottom. Filled side creates position; opposite stop becomes protective exit. Cancel leftover orders after exit; manual read-only plugin should describe intended order roles rather than submit orders. Box boundaries may move while forming, but must be frozen at eligibility/entry in causal research.
- Target distance is exact box width measured from entry, not from boundary. No trailing stop in baseline. Risk is width plus two ticks, reward width: reward/risk slightly BELOW1, despite confusing source phrasing about risk/reward a little over1:1. Costs lower payoff further.
- Independent timeframe plays: author gives intraday1/2/3/5/15min, swing60/120/240min/daily and favors currencies. Historical currency session-opening observations are not verified BTC effects.
- Visual examples confirm flexible discretionary treatment of a few outlier ticks. Must explicitly define boundary tolerance, test separation, lookback, box expiry, and competing signals before code. Cannot reconstruct this subjectivity by future chart appearance.
- Scaling-out variant PDF363 takes portions before final target. This changes realized expectancy; source assertion that it increases risk/reward is not universal. Do not conflate variant with single-target baseline.
- Source numeric issues: PDF354 short0.7583 minus54pips equals0.7529, while source target0.7530 is one pip different. PDF360 high140.03-low139.66 is37pips; source short139.65 to139.30 is35pips but text says36pips, inconsistent. PDF362 first high1.3784 is obvious scale error vs chart137.84. PDF349 refers to Figure14.4 while context is18.4. Do not silently inherit numbers.
- Historical Eurodollar liquidity/contract tick values, forex spread comparisons and claims of guaranteed forex fills are dated, broker/instrument specific, and unsuitable current specifications. Fixed pip-dollar conversions cannot be transferred to Delta contracts.
- Claims of high probability and illustrative winning trades provide no complete sample, costs, drawdown, or independent validation.

## Chapter19 HOLP/LOHP, PDF366–381
- Reversal framework after definitive trend, ideally new20-period extreme; author allows17/18 periods. HOLP high-of-low-period for bullish reversal; LOHP low-of-high-period for bearish.
- Bearish: track bar with highest high in trend, update to each fresh higher extreme; wait for a completed bar close below that extreme bar's low. Long mirror: close above high of lowest-low bar. Extreme cannot be chosen retrospectively. Confirmed close preferred over intrabar break, which can reverse and invalidate setup.
- Initial stop opposite extreme of signal extreme bar (short high, long low). Starting third period in trade, use two-bar trailing stop and close-based exit; examples reference lows of preceding two bars for longs/highs for shorts. Source says one example closes below 'close' of stop—ambiguous wording; don't infer a close-price stop silently.
- PDF379–381 weekly GBPUSD example qualifies special stop-hold rule: if advancing stop would place it already above current traded price for long after retracement, hold previous closing stop until new trend highs, then resume trailing. This needs exact state-machine formalization; do not claim a standard rolling2bar implementation is exact source replication.
- Entry examples use after session close fills; backtest must use causal next executable quote/bar or declared auction model, never assume exact historical close fill. Weekly/daily signals use chosen session/timezone, important BTC24/7 vs NSE regular sessions.
- PDF377 includes loss77pips and subsequent win171pips, lesson follow validated rule rather than emotional avoidance; does not justify every reentry without risk limits.
- Source bravado about $50k becoming$1m in leveraged weekly FX example is not a sizing rule. Do not downplay unbounded stock-short loss just because no stock reaches literal infinity (PDF366). Claimed universal all-market/timeframe efficacy is unvalidated.

## Transfer and validation boundary
These chapters support a research checklist: classify breakout vs reversal vs pullback; require explicit source-specific trigger, causal timestamps, frozen boundaries, entry execution assumptions, and preplanned exits; disclose discretionary undefined parts. Never turn a chart example into detected/live signal or profitability statistic. BTC requires own contract multiplier/tick, funding and24/7 session; NIFTY/BANKNIFTY require actual sessions and exact current option quotes, strikes, lots, IV/Greeks/spreads. Underlying target never directly converts to option premium without supported pricing. ₹1,000 per-trade cap from user preference remains independent of author leverage examples. No source methods have been empirically replicated, executed, or validated by this review. Any newly formalized setup should be labeled derived experimental until independently out-of-sample tested with all costs.
