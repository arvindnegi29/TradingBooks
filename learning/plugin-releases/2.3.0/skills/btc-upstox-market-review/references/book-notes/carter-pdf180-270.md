## Ch9: distinguish exhaustion from persistent participation
PDF180 is explicitly a US stock-market breadth setup: NYSE $TICK extremes are not BTC trade ticks, minimum price increment, trade count, or CVD. It fades extreme breadth in quiet/range conditions; later sections explicitly change to continuation trading when persistent extremes and failed retracements show a directional day. A general transferable idea is classify the regime before interpreting an extreme: an isolated excursion can behave differently from sustained one-sided participation. This is a hypothesis; author anecdotes do not establish a universal causal relation to institutional trading.

### Historical parameter specification
PDF180–181: take new fades only 10:00–15:30 US Eastern, excluding opening/closing half-hours. On +1000 $TICK short at market; on -1000 buy. A near miss such as+988 is not a signal. ES:3-point stop,2-point target; YM:30-point stop,20-point target. Both have35-minute time exit if neither price order hit. Stop fades for the day after two hard-stop losses (time exits do not count as hard-stop losses). After noon, if $TICK spent over85% of time above zero, pass further fades; at/after10:00 if all activity is one-sided, wait for opposite-sign activity before first fade. Text states the bullish filter explicitly; any symmetrical bearish implementation must be labeled an adaptation and pre-specified. The sampling method for calculating85% time above zero is not supplied; do not treat OHLC positive bars as identical to true elapsed time. Long/short stock-index proxies named include ES,YM,SPY,DIA,NQ/Russell; options preference ITM with delta>=0.70, not a verified premium-return rule.

The2:3 reward:risk requires >60% winners before costs if every outcome is exactly either target or stop. Time exits change that distribution, so60% is not a claimed measured required win rate for all examples. Costs and real-time trigger/fill latency matter substantially for scalps. No validated sample win rate, confidence interval, or holdout found.

## Ch10: daily trend pullbacks and ATR expansion, not unconditional band fading
PDF207 average-price zone uses13/21EMAs. PDF208 modified Keltner-style RTM bands:13period middle with bands1.5currentATR away. ATR14 is referenced; exact platform smoothing/middle-source needs explicit implementation choice. EMA is weighted history, not literally simple average of past13/21days; ATR true range includes previous-close gaps rather than merely high-low. PDF209 upward slope=>long pullbacks toward mean; exit toward upper band while often seeking only1ATR instead of full1.5ATR. Reverse idea for downtrends. Source describes stops outside opposite band; PDF210 also supplies generic2*ATR14 stop heuristic. These are alternative descriptions that require a chosen fixed rule before backtesting, not interchangeable after seeing outcome.

PDF209 optional half-size entry, add to predetermined full-size as price approaches opposite band, exit on return to mean with hard stop preserved. For plugin, never turn this into unrestricted averaging down. Only allow explicit precommitted scaling whose combined worst-case cash loss (including costs/gaps) fits user limit. Do not adopt author wording that it 'turns losers into winners' as reliable.

PDF210–212: author mostly uses daily, sometimes weekly RTM, explicitly not intraday. Author favors with-trend RTM; strong squeeze invalidates RTM-band containment assumption. Stop considering band fades while momentum release is active, resume when release loses momentum. This helps flag regime conflict, but cannot make unimplemented squeeze detector a verified veto. Daily RTM can miss entire strong moves. Alleged70%works/30%gives back all profits is narrative without methodology.

PDF211 momentum trade scaling alternative: first quarter at+1ATR, second at+2ATR and stop to entry, third at+3ATR, remainder until momentum weakens; bail if momentum fades before first target. This conflicts with Ch11 blanket 'do not trail stops'/pure momentum-exit rules; retain as separate management variants and do not mix ad hoc. Choose ATR reference time and whether targets update, because text leaves it unclear. Options daily squeeze expected1–3weeks; author uses delta>=0.70 and later month if only1week to expiry. These are historical preferences, not valid contract-selection defaults for Indian/BTC instruments.

PDF212 takes underlying4.50move *0.70delta=3.15premium move; identify this as simplified illustration, not executable forecast. Gamma/IV/theta, elapsed time and bid/ask change outcomes. Need actual contract prices/Greeks/scenario repricing for plugin.

PDF214–215 credit call spread example AMZN short240call/long245call, same expiry, credit2.30, US100share multiplier: max expiry grossprofit230per spread, grossloss270;100spreads23000/27000. Marked P&L14150 is snapshot, not realized performance. Stop on spread price3.50 is planned exit, not guaranteed fill.
