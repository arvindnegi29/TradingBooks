- Original scope is daily equity swing trades lasting days/weeks. Author wants8EMA above21EMA for longs; bid pullback to8EMA after cross, reverse for shorts. Weekly8>21 offered as long filter. These are author parameters, not universal BTC/NSE recommendations.

Watermark/trail behavior must be explicitly specified; example23 uses0.25% trigger(460). Never silently transplant percentages across instruments/timeframes.
- Entries repeatedly rearmed only after trend condition remains; after a failed short and price above8EMA, wait for a close back below8EMA before next rally-to8EMA short(444–445). Set orders/parameters prospectively, do not count a near-touch as fill(451). - Charts include EBAY,QCOM,KLAC,QLGC,CEPH,SBUX,GS,PSFT,TZOO,SNDK(441–451); several selected winners, explicit losses and trailing-stop losses also described. No full trade population, OOS test, costs or confidence interval supplied. EMA called order flow in prose(447–448), but price averages do not directly observe actual order flow or insiders.
- Later variant adds proprietary TTM Trend two opposite-color-bar exit; squeeze/C-wave can filter trend or manage runner(451–452). Base fixed8% target and optional half-at8%/runner are different strategy variants. Do not combine outcomes or pretend exact TTM formulas implemented merely from chart appearance.
- Options: source prefers ITM calls/puts where extrinsic share of option purchase price<30%, suggests abs delta around0.70+, near month unless less than two weeks remains(436–438). Here author calls extrinsic value 'premium'; actual quoted option premium is entire option price. Intrinsic call=max(S−K,0),put=max(K−S,0); extrinsic=option price−intrinsic. High delta and extrinsic share are related but not equivalent constraints. ITM does not remove theta/vega/spread/liquidity risk. Source says OTM universally bad; retain as author's preference, not fact. GOOG10calls at12 =12,000 with100share US multiplier, maximum paid-premium loss absent assignment/exercise complications(438–440); not applicable contract multiplier for India/BTC.
- SSF discussion(433–435) is historical US context:OneChicago,100shares,20%margin,thirdFriday,physical delivery,PDT/uptick claims. Must verify any modern products/rules from current official exchange/broker sources before use. Underlying liquidity does not guarantee derivative fill; never adopt claim that makers fill any reasonable order, no funding/interest cost, or chart-based stock/derivative prices always interchangeable. Lower posted margin is not lower economic risk.
