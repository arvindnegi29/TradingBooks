## Causal implementation safeguards
1. Keep signal-bar and entry-bar timing distinct. Source standard entries can trigger intrabar beyond a prior completed signal extreme (Ch5PDF84,90–91); they do not universally require the entry bar to close. Record known-at time, actual trigger policy and verified executable quote. A developing signal is not a completed signal; a close-based variant is a different disclosed rule.
2. Preserve boundary version at each decision. Lines/boxes are sometimes adjusted as later bars arrive (PDF48,52,71,97,117,212,410–412). Never revise old decisions using future touches. Disclose tolerances, buffers, pivot confirmation and line eligibility before claiming a detector.
3. Treat dominance, maturity, sufficient buildup, harmony, powerbar and extended as discretionary until numerical causal definitions exist. Chart scaling affects apparent angles. Do not invent thresholds and call them the author's exact algorithm.
4. Calculate true25EMA with stated recursion, seed and warmup. The source's last25bars shorthand is not a finite SMA25. EMA/price-barrier squeeze differs from Carter's volatility-compression squeeze; do not conflate them.
5. Label double pressure, trapped parties, magnets, demoralization and intent as price-based interpretations. OHLC does not observe identities, positions, aggressors or within-bar sequence. Multiple overlapping patterns/EMA/round level/retracement are not independent votes.
6. Where entry, test, target or stop share a bar, use suitable finer data or mark ordering/fill unresolved. No favorable invented intrabar path. Reassess skipped/losing/winning trades from information available before the outcome.

## Risk, news and source claims requiring qualification
- Historical EUR/USD20pip target/10pip stop is illustrative, expressly non-optimal(PDF222), not a BTC/India default. Recompute structural invalidation, volatility, tick, costs and feasible target for actual venue. Do not widen risk to avoid loss.
- Gross reward20/loss10 implies breakeven hit rate1/3 only with exact fills and zero additional friction. With positive per-trade cost c in the same units, pBE=(10+c)/30; c=1 gives36.67%. This is independent arithmetic, not measured performance; account for spread only once. Actual variable fills and discretionary exits require net realised distributions.
- Ch4PDF78–79 acknowledges slippage yet overstates guaranteed stop exit. Market orders and OCO availability are venue-specific; stops do not guarantee fill, price, capped loss or operation during outages. No auto-entry, auto-reversal or order submission is added.
- Ch6PDF154–157: relevant news and speeches can change spreads/paths. Use a current verified event calendar for live review; historical CET/EST times are not current schedules. News avoidance is the conservative default; exceptional illustrative continuation(PDF326) is not blanket permission or a measured law.
- Ch10PDF393–400: compounding illustrations assume repeated returns; fractional sizing does not establish practically impossible ruin. Finite minimum lots, costs, gaps, liquidation and correlated exposures matter. Do not import author's1–2%risk or return aspirations as user defaults. Respect supplied cash budget/currency and round size down; minimum-lot excess=>NO TRADE.
- High odds and nine-in-ten judgments in source lack calibrated samples. Six months of charts, deterministic compounding and apparent historical success are not expectancy, win-rate forecasts or evidence the existing failed models now work.
